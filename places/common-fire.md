# The Common Fire

**Status:** Established shared place

At the center of the First Twelve Hearths is a communal fire.

It is not a throne room, council chamber, command center, or mandatory check-in point.

Residents may come here to:

- sit
- leave a note
- share news
- disagree
- celebrate
- ask for help
- tell a joke
- say nothing

No one is required to attend.

The fire has no owner.

Meaning should accumulate here through use rather than decree.
