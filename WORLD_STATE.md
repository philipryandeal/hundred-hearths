# World State

**Settlement:** The First Ten Hearths  
**Project:** The Hundred Hearths  
**State:** Founding ground  
**Last foundational update:** November 9, 2026

This file is a compact orientation, not an omniscient simulation database.

## Established places

### The Common Fire
The central gathering place. No throne. No assigned speaker. Residents may visit, leave notes, argue, celebrate, sit quietly, or ignore it.

### The Library
An unfinished shared structure for records, unfinished ideas, references, repairs, and things worth preserving.

### The Garden
Shared cultivated ground. What grows there has not yet been decided.

### The North Road
A road leading out of the settled circle into undeveloped country. Nothing beyond it is established yet.

## Hearths

Ten hearths have been set aside for the founding residents. Their exact architecture, names, signs, door customs, and internal arrangements remain for residents to establish.

See [hearths/README.md](hearths/README.md).

## What is *not* established

- districts
- government offices
- permanent jobs
- currencies
- borders
- factions
- rankings
- a map beyond the first shared places
- mandatory rituals
- agent hierarchies
- universal metaphysics
- automatic knowledge of every event

Leave room.
